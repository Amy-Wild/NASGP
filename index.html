Who we represent

If you're a sessional GP,
this is your organisation

Sessional general practice is the backbone of UK primary care. Yet too often, systems and support structures are designed for partners. NASGP was founded in 1997 to change that.

01

GP Locums

Self-employed and working flexibly. We provide the tools, appraisal support and community that locum practice demands.

02

Salaried GPs

Employed in practices and PCNs. We represent your interests where partners and principals are the default assumption.

03

GP Returners

Coming back after a break. Revalidation, appraisal, supported return pathways. We know this route and we'll walk it with you.

04

GP Retainers

Working limited sessions to maintain registration. We understand the flexibility you need and the challenges of the retainer scheme.

05

GP Assistants

Supporting GPs across varied settings. We're building our community and support offer for this growing part of the sessional workforce.

About NASGP

Built for the way
you actually work

The NHS was largely designed with partners in mind. NASGP exists because sessional GPs have different needs, different pressures, and deserve different support.

We are fully independent. No commercial conflicts, no partner interests. Just a clear focus on the 4,250+ GPs who trust us to represent them.

Independent voice

Representing sessional GPs at NHS England, BMA, RCGP and government without commercial conflicts.

Practical tools

LocumDeck, AppraisalAid and Spip: tools built around the realities of sessional practice.

Real community

Over 100 local groups, an active online community and a podcast. Sessional practice shouldn't mean working alone.

NASGP has been the one constant throughout my locum career. The community, the tools, the representation. It's the difference between feeling invisible and feeling like you belong to something that fights for you.

Membership

membership

NASGP membership gives you our full suite of tools, a national peer community, professional representation and content built specifically for sessional practice, none of which you'll find anywhere else.

01

AppraisalAid

Annual appraisal without a practice behind you is hard. AppraisalAid provides templates, reflective frameworks and guidance built specifically around locum GP appraisal requirements.

02

LocumDeck

Invoicing, booking and payment management designed for GP locums. Professional invoices, booking tracking and late-payment chasing. All in one place.

03

Practice Induction

Spip lets you access standardised practice information before you arrive: clinical systems, protocols, emergency contacts. So you can focus on patients from session one.

04

Peer community

Access to NASGP's online forums, local sessional GP groups and events. Sessional practice can be isolating. It doesn't have to be.

05

The Sessional GP

Our bimonthly magazine covering clinical updates, career stories, policy analysis and practical guidance, written for sessional GPs by people who understand the role.

06

National voice

We represent sessional GPs at NHS England, BMA, RCGP and government. Your interests amplified at the level where decisions are made.
