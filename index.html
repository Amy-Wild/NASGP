Who we are

Built for the way you actually work

Sessional general practice is the backbone of UK primary care. Yet too often, the systems and support structures are designed with partners in mind — leaving locums, salaried GPs and returners navigating a complex landscape alone.

NASGP was founded in 1997 to change that. We are the independent national voice for sessional GPs — providing practical tools, professional community and representation at the highest levels of UK health policy.

Independent voice

We lobby on your behalf at NHS England, BMA, RCGP and government level — without commercial conflicts of interest.

Real community

Over 100 local sessional GP groups across the UK, with online forums, peer support and regular events.

Tools that actually help

From appraisal support to invoicing, practice induction to locum chambers — we build practical tools around how sessional GPs really work.

Who we represent

If you're a sessional GP, this is your organisation

01

GP Locums

Self-employed GPs working flexibly across practices. We're built around your needs — invoicing, appraisal, induction and chambers.

02

Salaried GPs

Employed within practices or PCNs. We represent your interests and provide the tools and community that partners take for granted.

03

GP Returners

Coming back to practice after a career break. We help with revalidation, appraisal, supported return pathways and peer connection.

04

GP Retainers

Working limited sessions to maintain registration. We understand the flexibility you need and support you through the retainer scheme.

05

GP Assistants

Supporting GPs in a variety of settings. We're growing our support for this important part of the sessional GP workforce.

Membership

Everything a sessional GP needs — in one membership

Membership of NASGP gives you access to our full suite of tools, a national community of peers, professional representation, and content built specifically for sessional practice.

AppraisalAid

Dedicated appraisal support built specifically for locum and sessional GPs. Templates, reflective frameworks and guidance that make annual appraisal manageable.

LocumDeck

Online invoicing, booking and payments designed for GP locums. Professional, fast and built around how you actually get paid for your sessions.

Practice Induction (Spip)

Access standardised practice information before you arrive. Spip means you walk in knowing the systems, protocols and contacts — ready to focus on patients.

Peer community

Access to NASGP's online community, forums and local sessional GP groups. Because sessional practice can feel isolated — and it doesn't have to.

The Sessional GP

Our bimonthly magazine covering clinical updates, career guidance, policy news and peer stories — written for sessional GPs, by people who get it.

National representation

We represent sessional GPs at NHS England, NHSEI, BMA, RCGP and government. Your collective voice, amplified at a national level.

Our tools

Practical tools that make sessional practice easier

We build and maintain tools designed around how sessional GPs actually work — not how the system assumes you work.

Appraisal

AppraisalAid

Annual appraisal is complex for GP locums — without a practice to organise your supporting information, it falls entirely to you. AppraisalAid provides structured templates, guidance and a dedicated reflection space built around the locum GP's unique appraisal requirements.

  • Templates built for sessional appraisal requirements
  • Reflective frameworks linked to Good Medical Practice
  • Guidance on evidencing your portfolio as a locum
Learn more
Practice induction

Spip

The Standardised Practice Information Portal. Spip lets practices share their essential information with locums before they arrive — clinical systems, protocols, emergency procedures and key contacts — so you can hit the ground running.

  • Standardised induction across all participating practices
  • Access before you arrive — no day-one surprises
  • Safer, more efficient sessions from day one
Learn more
Invoicing & booking

LocumDeck

The invoicing and booking platform built for GP locums. LocumDeck replaces the spreadsheets and chasing — giving you a professional, streamlined way to manage your bookings, issue invoices and get paid.
